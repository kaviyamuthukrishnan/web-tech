About Our College

Established in 2001, Sri Vidya College of Engineering is an autonomous institution affiliated with Anna University, committed to producing industry-ready engineers through quality education, research, and innovation.

Campus Facilities

  • Central Library with over 50,000 volumes
  • High-speed Wi-Fi campus
  • Modern computer and electronics labs
  • Sports complex and indoor stadium
  • Hostel facilities for boys and girls

Our Milestones

  1. 2001 - College established with 3 engineering branches
  2. 2008 - Achieved NAAC accreditation
  3. 2015 - Expanded to 8 engineering departments
  4. 2022 - Ranked among top 50 engineering colleges in the state

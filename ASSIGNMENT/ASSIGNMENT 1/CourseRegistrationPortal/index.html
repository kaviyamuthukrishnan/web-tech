    Registration guidelines & eligibility

    Registration guidelines

    • Enter your register number exactly as printed on your ID card.
    • Use your official university email address, not a personal one.
    • Only select courses offered for your current semester.
    • Check your total credits against your department's ceiling.
    • Submit before the registration window closes on 21 Sept 2026.

    Eligibility requirements

    1. You must be currently enrolled and in good academic standing.
    2. Prerequisite courses for your programme must be completed.
    3. Course combinations must comply with your department's rules.
    4. You may not exceed the permitted credit limit for the term.
